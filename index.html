How I can help

I am an experienced counsellor who qualified in 1996. I offer a comfortable, confidential space in Southport town centre where you can talk openly, be heard and feel understood. I also provide online and telephone counselling across the UK, and face-to-face sessions in other locations across Lancashire, Merseyside and Greater Manchester may be available nearer to you.

I have worked extensively with people experiencing anxiety, overthinking, low mood and depression, low self-esteem, relationship difficulties and phobias, as well as difficulties linked to internet or social media use.

I also have experience supporting people in demanding professional roles who may appear to be coping well while feeling stressed, overwhelmed or under constant pressure.

I have substantial experience working with autistic individuals, people with ADHD, and others who are neurodivergent.

How I work

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

CBT explores how thoughts, feelings and behaviours are linked. By recognising unhelpful patterns, you can develop more effective ways of responding to difficult situations and triggers. CBT is collaborative, so we work together to identify practical ways forward.

Person-centred counselling

I have diplomas in person-centred counselling and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy.

Alongside this, my person-centred approach provides a supportive, non-judgemental space where you can move at your own pace.

Mindfulness

I also draw on mindfulness where appropriate, helping you notice thoughts and feelings without becoming so caught up in them, and creating greater calm and perspective.

About me

I am a Registered Member of the BACP. My subsequent work has included mental health, rehabilitation, education and management. This has given me a strong understanding of psychological difficulties, workplace pressures and the challenges of maintaining balance when life becomes demanding.

My professional background includes over 30 years of experience across mental health, management and teaching.

I studied Philosophy and Psychology, followed by postgraduate study in Philosophy and Mental Health. From 2022 to 2025, I was a Research Fellow at Harris Manchester College, University of Oxford, where my research focused on mindfulness in education.
